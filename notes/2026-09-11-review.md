# Site review notes, Sep 11 2026

Working notes from the physics polish + full-site review session. For going over
together tomorrow.

## What changed today (late session)

- **Fixed-timestep physics.** The simulation now runs at 60 ticks per wall-clock
  second regardless of display refresh. Before this, a 144Hz monitor ran the
  whole ball story 2.4x too fast, which was the root cause of "the timing is not
  aligned" and "it's too fast to tell what happened".
- **Render interpolation.** The renderer draws between physics ticks, so high
  refresh screens get continuous motion instead of 60Hz steps.
- **Squash and stretch.** The ball flattens on impact (floor, walls, word
  landings, capped at ~36%), stretches along its direction of travel when
  moving fast, and springs back to round at rest. Verified by pixel-measuring
  the ball's bounding box: squash and stretch both render, resting shape is
  round within one antialiased pixel.
- **Catapult beat retimed and verified.** In-page recorder measured the word's
  actual rotation angle against the ball's departure frame. Gap went
  83ms -> 50ms -> 17ms -> 0ms across iterations. The launch tick (59) is bound
  to the CSS keyframes' real zero-crossing (~997ms, because of the snap
  segment's easing), not the keyframe percentage. Comments in both files bind
  the numbers together.

## Edge cases fixed

- **Webfont race**: word positions are measured only after `document.fonts.ready`
  (capped at 1.6s), so a slow Google Fonts load can no longer aim the story at
  where the words used to be.
- **Wrapped targets**: on narrow screens "University of Waterloo" wraps to two
  lines; the ball now aims at the first line fragment instead of the middle of
  the two-line bounding box.
- **Scroll-away mid-story**: scrolling off About while the story runs makes it
  concede: remaining words light, ball drops softly out of the way.
- **Grab mid-story**: snatching the ball ends the story cleanly (was already in,
  re-verified).
- **Hidden sections are `inert`**: links in the two invisible sections were
  still keyboard-tab-reachable. Now they're out of the tab order and AT tree.
- **Firefox scrollbars**: expanded sections that scroll internally now hide
  their scrollbar in Firefox too (`scrollbar-width: none` on `.stage-section`).

## Addendum, same day

- **Playback speed is 1.5x the original** via the single `SPEED` constant in
  App.tsx. The physics tick rate and the word-catapult CSS duration
  (1000 / SPEED ms) scale together, so the launch alignment holds at any rate.
  Re-measured at 1.5x: 0ms between the word's snap and the ball's departure.
- **The perch on X became a roll-off.** The ball lands carrying momentum,
  rolls across the top of X losing none of its motion (measured: 250ms on the
  word, position decreasing every frame, zero stationary frames), and falls
  off the left edge. The email sit also halved (70 -> 35 ticks) and the
  catapult base duration dropped 1500ms -> 1000ms, launch tick 59 -> 39.
- **Trail upgraded from dots to a comet ribbon.** One tapered, midpoint-smoothed
  path from tail to ball with a lengthwise fade, head bound to the interpolated
  ball position, opacity scaling with speed. Verified on both a slow drop and a
  hard throw.
- **Toggles removed.** Experience and project descriptions render in full,
  always. Projects flow down two CSS columns on desktop (all seven on one
  screen at 1440x900); a section taller than the screen scrolls internally.
  This also resolves open item 3: the Leap Tools numbers are now always
  visible.
- **Stage widened to max-w-6xl for the two-column Projects layout** (matching
  Zain's reference). About and Experience pin themselves to a 560px measure so
  prose line length is unchanged; only Projects uses the extra width.
- **Proportional stage.** The layout is designed against a 1440px canvas and
  scaled to the viewport with a JS-set transform (NOT CSS zoom: zoom's
  getBoundingClientRect semantics vary by browser, which briefly had the ball
  aiming off the words in the Cursor preview - fixed same day), so the
  whitespace ratio is identical on every screen (prose = 39% of width). When
  Projects is active the whole stage glides 127 design-px left so the wider
  sidebar+columns group is centred; About/Experience centre the narrow group.
  Projects' width is a fixed 810px in design space (same left edge as prose,
  extra space taken to the right). Zoom and slide are md+ only.

## Sep 12: fight v2 + verification tooling

- **Playwright added as a dev dependency** (headless only, no visible windows;
  chromium shell downloaded to the standard ms-playwright cache). Two untracked
  helper scripts, .pwtest.mjs / .pwfast.mjs, drive capture runs against the
  dev server on :3000; both are gitignored.
- **Fight rebuilt with two-bone IK** (elbows and knees are solved joints).
  Move set: jab, cross, kick, uppercut (launches the victim), flying kick,
  block, dodge, retreat-disengage, jab-into-cross combo. Landings absorb
  impact with a crouch scaled by fall speed, arms out, dust. KO bounces once,
  sprawls; winner raises arms; both fade.
- **Verified on camera**: flying kick silhouette, uppercut launch, extended
  cross with launched victim, Newsreader countdown numerals, KO sprawl + win.
  Fight length now ~10s. Two state-machine bugs found by tracing frames:
  cooldowns only decremented while idle (flying kicks could never trigger)
  and knockbacks too small to ever open leap distance.
- **Empty-space drag no longer selects text** (pointerdown on non-text targets
  is cancelled; drags starting on text still select). Verified both ways.
- Countdown font preloaded via document.fonts.load before the fight spawns.
- **Facing made provable, then made visible.** A window.__fight debug hook +
  a 400-sample Playwright assertion verify the invariant (grounded,
  non-committed fighters always face each other) across every state: 0
  violations. A one-tick staleness window on leaving committed states was
  found by the assertion and closed (squareUp runs at tick start and end).
- **Stickmen became humanoid robots.** Visor faceplate (front), antenna
  (back), chest plate with a forward core light, pelvis block, articulated
  joint pins, feet that point where they face, and depth-dimmed back limbs.
  The old design's facing cues were a 2px eye and a 9px fist offset, which is
  why fights kept reading as "backwards" even when the math was right.

## Sep 14: launch flash

- **Orange-then-fade on load fixed.** Not caching: the refactored playground
  dims the five words ~1-2s after first paint (fonts.ready + 450ms), so they
  rendered lit and visibly faded. They now render with the dim class in the
  initial markup (skipped under reduced motion), and Ball.dispose() no longer
  relights words (StrictMode's dev remount would have re-flashed them).
  Verified with a frame-zero sampler: first painted frame all grey, 0 rust
  frames pre-story, story still lights everything. Along the way my own patch
  briefly emptied HIT_LINK (shell ate a template literal) - caught by the
  same test, fixed with a proper edit.
- **Mid-air stall on section swap fixed.** The scroll handler interrupted the
  ball on every scroll event past 25% viewport, and interrupt clamped handoff
  velocity to ~180px/s - a ~1500px/s flight collapsed to a crawl, reading as a
  mid-air pause. Now scrolling only concedes the choreography (flight/windup/
  roll); a free ball is untouched, and story handoffs keep real momentum
  (clamp widened to +-800/+-600-800). Verified: 3 trials, airborne speed
  through the swap never dropped below 100% of pre-swap speed.
