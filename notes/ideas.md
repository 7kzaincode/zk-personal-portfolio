# Ideas backlog

For the ball, and for the site as a whole. Potential is how much it could add,
effort is how long it would take, risk is how it could make the site worse.

## Ball

1. **Throw the ball at a "more" row to expand it.**
   Play becomes navigation: fling it at a project title and that project opens.
   Potential: high (the one extension that deepens the existing idea instead of
   adding a second one). Effort: medium. Risk: low if hit detection is generous.

2. **Replay the story by clicking "Zain Khan".**
   The gesture existed once and is currently unused. Re-runs the whole
   entrance for someone who missed it.
   Potential: medium. Effort: low. Risk: none.

3. **Arrow keys nudge the ball.**
   A tiny keyboard easter egg for the kind of person who tries it.
   Potential: medium for the right audience. Effort: low. Risk: must not eat
   arrow-key scrolling; only nudge when the ball is at rest.

4. **A quiet bounce sound, defaulted off.**
   One soft felt-like thock, with a small mute toggle.
   Potential: medium. Effort: low. Risk: high if ever on by default; browsers
   also block audio before first interaction, which the toggle solves.

5. **Second ball on double-click, capped at two.**
   Two balls can collide, which is instantly fun.
   Potential: medium. Effort: medium (ball-ball collision). Risk: clutter,
   starts to feel like a toy site rather than a portfolio with a toy.

6. **Ball perches on the section heading when you change sections.**
   It notices where you are: hops onto "Experience" when you arrive.
   Potential: high charm. Effort: medium-high (targets change per section,
   crossfade interplay). Risk: could get in the way of reading; needs care.

   Potential: high. Effort: low.

14. **Custom 404** in the same voice, with the ball resting mid-page.
